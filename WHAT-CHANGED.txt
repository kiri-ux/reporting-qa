1. THE HUNDRED EXTRA 7 MOUNTAINS LIFETIMES - MY BUG
----------------------------------------------------
Build 72 read "cancelled or complete" off a SINGLE line item and put a lifetime
on the board for the whole client.

That is wrong twice over. A cancelled line item inside a live order is an
ordinary thing - one product got dropped, the rest of the campaign is running.
And every campaign that has ever finished sits at "IO Complete" forever, so any
client with one old finished line and a stale end date suddenly owed a
close-out report.

Closing a campaign out is a statement about the CLIENT, not about one line of
their order. The flag now only earns a lifetime when nothing that client has is
still running. 45911 still gets its lifetime, because all four of its lines are
complete. The hundred others go away.

Nothing to press - the board is computed live, so the count is right as soon as
this deploys.


2. PAUSED ORDERS ARE ON THE BOARD
----------------------------------
53392 and 54937 were on nobody's board because a paused header is not a live
one and the import threw the whole order away. A campaign paused on the 20th
delivered for nineteen days, and those days are owed a report.

Paused orders are now kept and land on the board on the strength of their
dates. A paused product is still not EXPECTED on the report and still not a
surprise when it turns up - that rule is unchanged.

This adds rows. It has to: the export gives a flight, not the day somebody hit
pause, so the board cannot tell paused-on-the-2nd from paused-on-the-30th.
