YOU WERE RIGHT ABOUT RETHINK, AND THE LIST WAS WRONG
======================================================
Order 4701, Memorial Hospital, ReThink Media Group. IO Live, dated 2018-11-01
to 2026-12-31. Its four oldest line items are IO Complete and finished between
2021 and 2023. The three live ones start 2021-08-11 and 2024-01-03.

The page asked for the earliest start among line items marked LIVE. That is
2021-08-11, which is after the 2,000-day cutoff, so ReThink came out as "one
pull" and sat in the middle of the table instead of at the top.

Both halves of that question were wrong.

  LIVE IS NOT THE TEST. A line that is paused, cancelled or IO Complete still
  gets reported on - a cancelled campaign is owed a lifetime covering its whole
  history, and none of that was in the range.

  AND THE LINE ITEM IS NOT THE THING. TapClicks filters the export on the line
  item's start date, but what has to be covered is the ORDER. When 4701 ends,
  its lifetime reaches back to 2018 - and the check that says "this lifetime
  does not go back to the campaign start" would have been comparing against a
  start date three years too late.

So the question is now: which orders are still being reported on, and how far
back do they go. An order still open, or that ended recently enough to still
owe a report, needs its whole history in the pull - line items that finished
years ago included, because they are part of what its lifetime covers. The date
is the earliest ORDER start, not the earliest live line.

An order that genuinely finished long ago is still excluded, so this does not
drag the range back to 2011 for everybody. The test for that is on the order's
end date now rather than a flag on one line.

number, and each one is still a narrow slice of one partner's history rather
than another copy of the board.
