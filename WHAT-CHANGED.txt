"JULY 2026", NOT "2026-07"
=============================
The period is stored as "2026-07" because it sorts and compares, and that is
the right way to keep it. It was also being printed straight at people in a
dozen places, on the same screens as a board heading reading "July 2026
reports" - the same month under two names, one page.

One filter now, used everywhere a person reads a month:

  the cycle dropdown, top right of the board and the links page
  the report viewer's head line, "Bloom Heating · July 2026"
  the order panel, "delivered impressions in July 2026"
  a report's own order list - the column head, the "ran in", the fold
  the batch page, the dashboard, the lifetimes table
  "lifetime already delivered in May 2026" on the not-owed panel
  "not running in July 2026" on a product finding's trace
  the batch digest

Anything that is not a period comes through untouched, so it is safe on a
column that might be blank or hold "Lifetime". URLs and form values keep the
hyphenated form, which is what they need.

Two tests hold it: one on the filter, one that reads the templates and fails
if a period is printed at a person without it.


TWO RULES THAT WERE TOO SHORT TO READ
========================================
Both said the right thing in too few words.

  One product meeting that is enough for the client's report.

is now

  One product is enough. The report covers the whole client, so if any one of
  its products hits 7 days, the client is owed a report - the others do not
  have to.

and

  Short runs are not exempt. An order that ended on the 2nd owes one.

is now

  How long it ran does not matter. An order that ended on the 2nd still owes
  a lifetime.

Same rules. No behavior changed.
