TWO NAMES FOR ONE CLIENT IS NOW AN ALERT
===========================================
You confirmed it, so the panel says it.

Build 105 taught the match to take a trailing product off the client name, so
"A-1 Appliance" and "A-1 Appliance - Display" stopped being two clients. That
fixed the false "no order behind it" flag, but it was worded as a note in
passing: matching works, worth ruling out the other thing.

The other thing is what it turned out to be. The delivery data was on the plain
record and the order was on the product one. So the panel is a warning now, in
gold, and it says what to go and check rather than what to rule out:

  Check N clients: the delivery data may be linked to the wrong client record.

The list opens by itself instead of hiding behind "Show the N", with the three
columns as before - partner, what the serving file calls it, what the order tool
calls it. It stays quiet when the two tools agree, which is the whole point: a
flag that is always on gets ignored.

It fires on the shape, not on proof, so some of these will be fine. The wording
says "may be" for that reason. But every one of them is a client where the two
systems hold two different names for the same business, and that is worth thirty
seconds each.
