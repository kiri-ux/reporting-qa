AMERICAN ENGLISH, EVERYWHERE
===============================
The one you caught - "a product nothing can recognise" on the Order list page -
and every other one with it. Fifty-odd words across twenty-one files:

  colour/colours/coloured    recognise/recognisable    grey
  cancelling                 authorise/authorises      labelled
  judgement                  itemised                  memorised
  catalogue                  centred                   whilst
  towards                    afterwards                maths
  normalise                  organise                  analyse

Code as well as screen text, because half of what a person reads on screen is
written in a Python string, and the comments are read by whoever comes to fix
this next. One function was renamed too - pace.humanise is humanize.

names and order statuses copied out of your export - "Cancelled" as the IO
tool spells it, a client with "Centre" in its name - and correcting somebody
else's data is a different thing from writing in one voice. The export says
what it says.

naming the file, the line and what to write instead. It cannot drift back.
