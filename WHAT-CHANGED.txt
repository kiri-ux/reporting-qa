1. THE MONTH CANNOT REPORT MORE THAN ITS OWN LIFETIME
------------------------------------------------------
New check. When a client is getting both reports in the same cycle, the month
is compared against the campaign that contains it. More impressions or clicks
in the month than in the whole campaign means one of the two was pulled with
the wrong date range - and since they go out together, in one folder, to one
person, they will see it before we do.

  Fires only on the pair. A client getting one report is never compared
  against anything, and a figure that could not be read off a report is not
  compared either - "not read" is not zero.

  Half a percent of slack. TapClicks prints these rounded and the two reports
  are pulled minutes apart, so a sliver of daylight is the tool, not a mistake.

  It shows on both reports and names the other one, so whichever you open first
  tells you the same thing and says what to open next.

Every re-check picks this up automatically - the checks fingerprint changed, so
reports already on the board get re-read without anybody pressing anything.
