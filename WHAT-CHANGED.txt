TEXOMA ROOFING - A LIFETIME ROW WAS GETTING A MONTHLY'S REASON
==============================================================
"Live Chat does not earn a report on its own" is a true sentence about the
wrong question. Whether a product earns a report of its own decides a MONTHLY.
A lifetime is about a campaign finishing, and that check was running first and
answering for it.

It does not run on a lifetime row now. And the lifetime answer says the second
half, which was the part you actually wanted:

  order 54263 does not end until 2026-12-31 and overlaps order 54900, which is
  still running - so this client is reported on that order rather than closed
  out here

"Runs past the window" said why no lifetime is owed and not what happens
instead. The client IS on the cycle; they are on the other order's report. It
names that order.


SPEND PRODUCTS USE THE AD SPEND FIELDS
======================================

Max each have their own spend column and the reader has always preferred it.
What was missing was the campaign total on those lines, for the same reason
impressions were missing: nothing was multiplying by the months.

where they had none, worked out the same way - monthly spend times
months_running - and only ever filling a blank, never replacing a total the
export gave.

  Blessing Health System   LinkedIn   $1,750/mo  x44    $77,000

Impressions and spend now both come from the order's own month count.
