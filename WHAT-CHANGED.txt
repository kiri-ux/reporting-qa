5. "STARTING" FOR SEVERAL MINUTES ON A JOB THAT HAD FINISHED
--------------------------------------------------------------
The row reloaded itself with a meta refresh written into a list item. A meta
refresh belongs in the document head; inside a list item most browsers ignore
it. So the page sat on the first frame of a job that was already done - the one
thing a progress indicator must never do.

It asks the server every three seconds now, updates the count in place, and
reloads the moment nothing is running.
