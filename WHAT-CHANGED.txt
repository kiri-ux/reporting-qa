THE BREAKOUT SHEET IS READ LIVE
==================================
Yes, and nothing new had to be built for it.

The tool already authenticates to Google with a refresh token and the full
Drive scope, because that is how it packages reports into Drive - and a Google
Sheet is a Drive file. So the sheet is exported as CSV through the credentials
that are already there. No service account, no extra scope, no new dependency,
nothing to authorise.

  Sheet   1_WfyDOEN4oOdPdEoYtv5yMZZ6_hk8vu5o6pElIJ-8d4
  Tab     Market Breakout (the first, which is what Drive's export gives)

It reads on every order sync, and only when the sheet has actually changed -
the CSV is checksummed, so an unedited sheet costs one request and no writes.

ONE THING TO CHECK ON YOUR SIDE: the sheet has to be visible to the Google
account the Drive connection authorised as. If it is not, the Partners page
says so in those words rather than failing quietly, and the roster already
loaded is untouched.

Paste a different URL into ROSTER_SHEET to point it elsewhere - the id and the
tab are read out of a normal browser URL. Blank turns the whole thing off.


TWO GUARDS, BOTH FROM THINGS THAT HAVE ALREADY GONE WRONG HERE
=================================================================
IT WILL NOT REPLACE THE ROSTER WITH SOMETHING THAT IS NOT ONE. A read that
comes back as a Google sign-in page, or empty because a tab was renamed, parses
perfectly well as "a roster with no partners in it" - and importing that
deletes 206 partners and every owner on the board with them. A read has to
carry at least two thirds of what is already loaded or it is refused, with the
count in the message. A real batch of leavers still loads; a permissions page
never does.

A DELIVERY TARGET SET IN THE TOOL IS NOT BLANKED BY A SHEET THAT IS SILENT
ABOUT IT. That field is set on the Partners page and not always in the sheet,
and a blank means Drive - which is how a Dropbox partner's client got a Drive
link once already. When the sheet names a target the sheet wins; when it says
nothing, what is here is kept. Uploading a CSV by hand still replaces outright,
which is what an upload is for.

AND IT NEVER WRITES BACK. The sheet stays the source of truth for who owns
what. A test fails if any writing call turns up in that file.
