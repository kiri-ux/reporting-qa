5 PREVIEWS, NOT 4
====================
Wine and Design Newport News' CTV grid has five creatives. Four print
"Thumbnail not available". The fifth prints NOTHING AT ALL - an empty preview
cell - which is the same failure with nothing to count.

The text cannot tell that cell apart from one holding a WORKING thumbnail: an
image is empty in pdftotext exactly like an empty cell is. So this one is
decided on the rendered pixels, the same way the ad-screenshot check already
works - a cell holding a real ad has thousands of colors, an empty one has the
table fill and its border.

Checked against all seven fixture reports: no new findings on any of them,
which is right - every preview cell in them either holds an image or says
"Thumbnail not available" already.


THE FILTERS ONLY FILTERED THE PAGE YOU WERE ON
=================================================
Two bugs, both of which made a saved view show the wrong thing.

FIRST: those filters were browser state and nothing else. The JS read the URL,
ticked the boxes, and hid cards - and the cards it can hide are the twenty on
screen out of a hundred and forty-five. So a view for Lockwood opened on page
one, found no Lockwood card among the twenty, and hid all twenty. A named view
that reliably showed nothing.

The server reads them now - partner, buyer, reporter, trainer, status, only -
and narrows BEFORE the page is cut, so a filter reaches the whole cycle. The
menus are still built from every group rather than from what survived, because
offering only what is still showing means one pick and the menu can never take
you anywhere else. Picking one reloads; typing in the search box still filters
instantly without one, and a pick sends you back to card one, because the page
you were on is a page of the list you just replaced.

SECOND: the Status menu offered every REPORT state - Not received, Errors, In
review, Good to go - and a CARD is labelled "Good to go" or "Open". So every
option in that menu but one matched no card at all and emptied the board. It
offers the two a card actually carries now.

And what the board is narrowed to is printed as chips on the filter row, so a
view that filters to one partner stops looking like a cycle with one partner
in it.
