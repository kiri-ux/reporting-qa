1. "How can we fix these once and for all" - here is the actual cause
----------------------------------------------------------------------
It is one bug, and it has been behind most of the product findings you have
been sending me.

expected_products matched a client's orders BY ACCOUNT ID, and only fell back
to matching by name IF THE ID MATCH FOUND NOTHING:

    hit = [lines matching the report's order id]
    if not hit:
        hit = [lines matching the client name]

River Valley Builders' report carries order 31050. The id match found
something, so the name match never ran - and their live Live Chat order, which
sits under 31171, was invisible. The report was then failed twice: once for a
Live Chat "with no live order" that was sitting right there, and once for two
products that had stopped in 2024, which were the only orders anybody was
looking at.

That is also what happened with 43619 back at the start, and it is why these
keep coming back in different costumes - I kept fixing the symptom.

Every order for the client now counts, matched by id OR by name, together.

And so this is self-diagnosing next time rather than costing three rounds:
Investigate on a product finding now lists every order that was considered, its
dates, and whether it counted:

    Live Chat · order 31171     2024-07-17 to 2026-12-31 · counted
    Meta · order 31050          2024-12-06 to 2026-07-31 · counted
    Mobile Conquesting · 31050  2023-05-05 to 2024-04-30; 2024-05-01 to
                                2024-06-30 · not running in 2026-07
    Social Mirror · order 31050 2024-07-17 to 2024-11-30 · not running in 2026-07

If one of these is still wrong, that panel says which order and which date is
being read wrong, and I can fix it from the screenshot.


2. Field Of Dreams: Display is the Mobile Conquesting buy
-----------------------------------------------------------
Reported three times, and right every time. One product on the order, and a
report carrying a Display slice, a "Field Of Dreams - AI Display" line item and
a Display Creative Performance widget - all of which IS Mobile Conquesting. The
order calls the product "Mobile Conquesting Display & Video Ads"; Display and
Video are the formats it is delivered in.

A product on the report is no longer rogue when an ordered product is what
prints it. The list is deliberately narrow - only the products whose order name
literally says "Display & Video Ads" (Mobile Conquesting, Meta, TikTok, DOOH,
Performance Max) plus Native Display. Social Mirror and CTV are not on it, so
this does not quietly stop catching real ones.

It only ever FORGIVES a product on the report. A Mobile Conquesting order does
not owe a Display section.
