NO, IT DID NOT SAVE. IT DOES NOW.
====================================
"Check a list" was a form post and nothing else. Paste four hundred rows of the
tracker, read the comparison, refresh - and it is gone, or the browser offers to
send the whole thing again.

The list is kept per cycle now. Come back to the page and it is there, and it
re-runs against the board AS IT IS THEN - which is the way this actually gets
used: read it, go and fix three rows, look again.

It says so under the button, because a thing that saves silently is a thing
nobody trusts to have saved:

  Kept for August 2026, pasted by Kiri · Aug 31 at 4:55 PM ET. It is here when
  you come back, and it re-runs against the board as it is then.

There is a "Clear the list" button beside it. One list per cycle, overwritten -
it is a copy of somebody else's spreadsheet, not a record.


AND EVERY ROW ON IT IS NOW A DECISION
========================================
"On the list, not on the board" is a question, not a finding. Some of those
rows belong on the board and the rules missed them; some are on the tracker by
mistake. Both need somebody who knows the client to say which - and to say it
ONCE, rather than working the same forty rows out again next month.

Each row has a note box and two buttons:

  APPROVE   puts the row on this cycle's board, with your note as the reason it
            is there. It is the same override as "Needs a report" on the
            not-owed panel, so it survives every rule underneath it and the row
            says who kept it there.

  REJECT    settles it. The row stays on the table with the note on it rather
            than reading as an open question the next time somebody opens the
            page.

Both record who and when, and both can be undone.

ONE HONEST LIMIT. Approve can only put back a client the ORDER EXPORT knows
about - the board is built from that export, so a client nothing in it carries
cannot have a row created for it. Those are the rows whose reason reads "no
order line carries 51981 - it is not in the export, or the export is out of
date". The decision is still recorded; the row cannot appear until the order
does. The page says that under the table rather than letting Approve look like
it worked.

The partner comes from the order line rather than from your list, because the
list carries a partner CODE ("7MOU SG", "LOCK AUG") and the board is keyed on
the partner's real name. Matched on the order id, which is the one thing both
sides agree on.
