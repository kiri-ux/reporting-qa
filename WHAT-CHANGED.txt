1. Pacing: a full month against a full month's budget
-------------------------------------------------------
New check. It reads the spend the report itself prints and compares it to what
the order says that product should spend in a month. More than 50% adrift
either way is a warning:

    PPC spend is 60% under budget
    The report shows $400.00 against a monthly budget of $1,000.00. Half a
    month's budget adrift is usually a reporting fault rather than a media one -
    a wrong date range, a line item missing from the pull, or two flights added
    together.

Where the numbers come from:

  - Spend: the Spend Overview tiles - "PPC Ad Cost", "LinkedIn Ad Cost",
    "Performance Max Cost". Read off the TILE rather than by adding up a cost
    grid, because the grid is a top-N list on most reports so its total is not
    the campaign's.
  - Budget: Monthly Campaign Budget on the order, summed across the line items
    that were actually running that month. Paused lines and other months do not
    count.

Four things it will not do:

  - A LIFETIME report is never paced. It covers a whole flight and a monthly
    budget says nothing about it.
  - A product whose spend the report does not print is skipped, not failed.
    Most products print no spend at all.
  - A line with no budget on file contributes nothing rather than zero. "No
    budget loaded" and "a budget of nothing" are different claims and only one
    of them is true.
  - With no budgets loaded for a client the check shows as skipped.

One subtlety worth knowing about: two tiles share a line ("PPC Ad Cost" and
"PPC Cost-Per-Click") and their values share the line underneath, so taking the
first dollar amount gives the cost-per-click as often as the cost. The value is
matched to its tile by COLUMN. Verified against the everything-sample: PPC
$4,037.06, LinkedIn $562.37, Performance Max $7,027.70, and the cost-per-click
correctly read as $0.96 rather than mistaken for a cost.


2. Pull range by partner
--------------------------
Yes - and this is worth doing.

TapClicks filters the export on the line item's START date, not on delivery, so
one campaign that began in 2018 and is still running forces the whole range
back to 2018. But that is one date for a hundred and forty-six partners, and
almost none of them need it.

There is a "Pull range by partner" link on the Orders page now. It gives, per
partner, the earliest start date of a line item that is STILL RUNNING, oldest
first, with a count of live line items. A campaign that finished in 2011 does
not need to be in the pull and no longer sets anybody's date.

Open it and the top few rows are the partners genuinely forcing an old range.
Pull those on their own and the rest in a narrow window, and a daily sync stops
being a couple of million rows.
