CHECKS CAN BE SWITCHED OFF. Reporting flags on the Rules page now has an
On/Off switch against every check. Off means it stops running and everything it
has already flagged stops counting - on the board, in the status, in the
filters - the moment you flip it. Nothing is re-read.

Editing a check that is off costs nothing. The re-check queue is driven by a
hash of the checking code, which is why every change to any rule put the whole
board in it; a switched-off check is not part of that hash any more.

Switching one back ON does queue a re-check, and should: that rule never ran on
anything judged while it was off.

HOLD RE-CHECKS. Same page, top of the flags tab. Held, the sweep does not start
on its own - the count of what is behind stays on screen with a button to let
it go when you want it. Use it on an afternoon when a rule is being worked on.

Findings of the same kind are one line in the filter. A row's CTR, a tile's CTR
and the top-line CTR were three entries named after whichever report got there
first - "Meta is 57% short", "3 creative previews did not render". The names
are written now, one per kind, with no report's figures in them.

Social Mirror ad sizes: only 300x250, 300x600, 320x480 and 336x280 count.

"Ordered but not on the report" no longer fires on an SEO report, and the
re-check leaves SEO reports alone the way the upload always has.
