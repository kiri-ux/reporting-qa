THE MINUTE
=============

box is busy, and I am fairly sure I am what made it busy.

stamped with the fingerprint of the rules that judged it, and when that changes

LinkedIn fix reaches the reports it already got wrong. But that is eight
hundred PDFs through pdftotext on a box that is also trying to draw pages, and
on a small instance the pages lose.

  842 reports are being re-checked in the background. A deploy changed the

A slow tool with a reason and a number coming down is a different thing from a
slow tool.

IT TREADS MORE CAREFULLY WHEN THE QUEUE IS LONG. The sweep rested for as long
as each batch took, capped at twenty seconds - about half the box. Over two
hundred reports it now rests twice as long as it works, capped at forty-five.
It takes longer and nobody is waiting on it: those reports have been on the
board for hours already.
