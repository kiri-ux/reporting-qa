SO: TWO THINGS THAT MAKE IT CHEAPER, AND ONE THAT MAKES IT VISIBLE
==================================================================
SLOW PAGES NOW WRITE THEMSELVES DOWN. Anything over 3 seconds gets a row in the
database - both workers, kept, readable the next day. You do not have to catch
it happening any more. It is at the bottom of "Why is it slow".

AND THE SECONDS ARE SPLIT. Every page is now timed in two halves: working the
answer out, and drawing it. A four-second page with a fifth of a second in the
database is one of two completely different problems and the total cannot tell
them apart - which is how the last two builds got aimed at the wrong one.

THE BOARD STOPPED RE-DECIDING THE SAME NAMES. Shortening "Lauren Hunter" to
"Lauren" has to know whether another Lauren exists, and that is a fact about
the roster - 150 names that do not change between one label and the next. It
was being worked out from scratch on every call: 1,964 times for one page.
A third of the time the board spent building itself. It is worked out once now.

If your box is four times slower than mine, a third off the build is real. It
is not a minute.
