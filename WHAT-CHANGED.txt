"125 PARTNERS HAVE NO ORDER LINES" WAS CRYING WOLF
=====================================================
You are right that it means nothing - most of a hundred and fifty-eight
partners simply have nothing running, and no roster comparison can tell that
apart from a file that did not land.

THE SERVING FILE CAN. A client that DELIVERED impressions this month and has
no order line at all cannot be a campaign that went dark, and cannot be a
spelling the two tools disagree on: something was running and the order list
has nothing to judge it against. That is precisely what a partner's export
failing to land looks like.

So the panel is that now: "N clients delivered impressions in 2026-07 but have
no order line loaded", with the partner, the client and the days each of them
served. Clients with a single day are left out - one day is as likely to be a
stray row as a campaign.

If that panel is empty, every partner that served this month has orders
loaded, and the old whole-board export has nothing left to give.
