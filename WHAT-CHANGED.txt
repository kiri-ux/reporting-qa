ADD A ROW BY HAND - AND SEE THE ONES YOU ADDED
==============================================
New panel on the cycle page, just above Reports: "N rows added by hand this
cycle."

It answers both questions at once. Every row somebody put on this cycle
themselves is listed there - partner, client, kind, order number, who and when,
the note, and whether the report has come in yet - with an undo arrow on each.
Approving from the list check has always written exactly this override, so your
approves show up here too. That was the missing half: an approve that worked
and an approve that silently did nothing looked identical, and the only way to
check was to already know the client's spelling and search for it.

Under the list is the form:

  Partner | Client | Report (monthly / lifetime / SEO) | Order | Why

That is the spot for 53206. C & W Roofing is cancelled, ran earlier in the
year, and is owed a lifetime nobody has pulled - there is no rule that finds
that from an export with nothing running in August, and there does not need to
be one. There needs to be somewhere to say it.

The row behaves like any other from then on: it waits for its report, gets
checked, gets signed off, and counts toward the partner being ready. This cycle
only - next month starts from the export again.

The order number rides along now, so searching 53206 finds the row. Without it
a hand-added row is findable only by the client's name, which is the spelling
the two tools disagree about in the first place.
