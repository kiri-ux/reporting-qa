HOW TO REVERSE AN APPROVE
=========================
The small link under the name and date in the Call column. It said "undo",
which was not findable - you asked - so it now says "Undo approval" or "Undo
rejection" and its tooltip says what that does to the board. As of yesterday
it also reaches the board properly: undoing an approve takes the row back off
this cycle.


YOUR CANCELLATION RULE
======================
Cancelled at the LINE ITEM and cancelled at the ORDER were one flag, so a
single cancelled line took the whole client's monthly off the board. They are
two flags now and the rule is yours:

  * a cancelled line under a live order - the monthly is still owed, and the
    cancelled product still appears on it, because it ran for part of the month
  * the whole order cancelled, having run in the month - lifetime, no monthly

AND A CANCELLED LINE CANNOT ASK FOR A REPORT BY ITSELF. Both halves of that
come from the same place: nothing in the export says WHEN somebody hit cancel,
only what the line was sold to run to. So a cancelled line dated across the
month may have delivered all of it or none of it. Joining a report that is
being pulled anyway is safe; being the sole reason to pull one is not.

Order 51378 is exactly why. One live product (Website Visitor ID, which never
appears on a report), one starting in September, and seven cancelled or
complete lines - one of them dated to 15 September. That one cancelled line was
asking for a monthly for a client with nothing to report on, and the board could
only say "worth a closer look". It now asks for nothing.


NORTH BAY TIP - THE DATE IN THAT MESSAGE WAS NOT THAT ORDER'S
=============================================================
You were right, and it is worth knowing why, because it is structural.

An order line row is one client and one product rolled up across every order
behind it, and its end date is the LATEST of them. North Bay TIP has:

  54783  IO Complete  ends 13 August      <- the one you asked about
  55433  IO Live      ends 26 September

So the row said 26 September, and the message reported that as "the campaign"
about an order that finished six weeks earlier. The per-order detail is on the
row all along; it just was not being asked. That row now reads:

  order 54783 ends 2026-08-13, inside this cycle's lifetime window - but
  another order for this client is still running, so the lifetime waits until
  the campaign is finished. Approve it here if this one should be reported now.

A QUESTION FOR YOU, AND I HAVE DELIBERATELY NOT GUESSED AT IT. There is a rule
that an ended order overlapping a running one waits - the reasoning being that
the client is not dark, the buy just changed shape. That is what is holding
54783 back. If a finished ORDER should get its lifetime regardless of whether
another order is running, say so and I will change it. It is a one-line rule
and it moves a lot of rows, so I would rather you told me than have me decide.
