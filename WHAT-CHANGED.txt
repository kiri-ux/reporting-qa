1. The logo check was guessing, and the guess was wrong
--------------------------------------------------------
Ken Waddell's report carries the SEVEN MOUNTAINS logo. It is correct. It got
failed anyway, because the rule was: a logo on three or more markets cannot be
any one partner's, so it must be the tool's.

Seven Mountains runs 7 Mountains PA, 7 Mountains PA Altoona and 7 Mountains KY
as separate markets on this board, and prints one logo across all of them. Any
threshold that catches the tool's default also catches every group covering
more than a couple of markets. There is no number that works.

SO IT NO LONGER GUESSES. It is told, once.

On any report page there is now a PAGE-ONE LOGO block showing the exact crop
the check compares - the picture, not the fingerprint - with one button:

    [This is the tool's default logo]

Press it on any report carrying the generic blue chart icon and every report
with that mark fails from then on, including the ones already on the board:
they are queued for a re-check the moment you mark it. Nothing else fails.
"It is a real logo" takes it back.

Until you mark one, the check has nothing to compare against and shows as
skipped rather than guessing. So on this deploy it goes quiet until you point
at the bad one - one click, and no false positives to argue with afterwards.
