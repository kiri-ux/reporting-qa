"GOOD TO GO ONLY" - IT EXISTED, AND IT WAS IN THE WRONG PLACE
===============================================================
Build 82 added it, but only on partners that had ALREADY been packaged. The
other list on that page was "ready, not packaged", which meant every report
finished.

So a partner two reports into twenty was on neither list. Which is exactly the
partner you want to send the finished two for.

That list is now "Not packaged yet" and holds any partner with something signed
off and nothing sent, whether it is finished or not:

  all done      [Package & share]
  2 of 20 done  [Send the 2 good to go]

Nothing is deleted and nothing is lost. The reports still being worked on stay
out of the folder until they are signed off, and go up on the next sync
alongside what is already there. The partner's link never moves either way.

Already-packaged partners keep the same button beside Sync reports.


AND THE FLAG THAT WOULD HAVE NAGGED YOU ABOUT IT
==================================================
Sending only the finished ones is deliberate, so the ones left behind must not
count as "changed since this was packaged" - that would turn the needs-a-sync
flag on and leave it on for the rest of the cycle, which is the fastest way to
teach somebody to ignore it.

A report is behind when it was filed and has since moved, or when it is signed
off and has never been filed. Not when it is still being worked on.
