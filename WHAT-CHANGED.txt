I FIXED THE WRONG REPORTER FILTER
=================================
There are two on that page and they are different things. 175 fixed the one on
the partner cards. Taylor is using the one on the REPORTS table, four rows
lower, and your address bar says so - the link he clicked reads

  /cycle?period=2026-08&page=2#reports

with no filter on it at all, because that filter was never in the address in
the first place.

The card filters have been server-side for a while: pick one, the page reloads
narrowed, and the choice is in the URL. The report table's columns - Partner,
Kind, Status, Reporter - were doing something else entirely. They filtered the
fifty rows the browser happened to be holding. So page 2 is a fresh page of the
UNFILTERED board, and the dropdown honestly reads "All" when you land on it,
because that is the truth about the page you are now looking at.

That also means the filter never saw the other 970 reports even before you
paged. "3 of 50 rows" on a cycle of 1,020 is the same trap the search box was
in a while back.

They narrow the whole cycle now, the same as the card filters. Picking one
reloads the board filtered, the choice is in the address, the pagers carry it,
and page 2 is page 2 of YOUR reports.

It filters on the row's own value rather than on what the cell prints - a Kind
cell also carries the flight dates, and a filter built out of the printed text
was offering "lifetime 2026-01-01 to 2026-08-15" as a thing to pick.
