"1 STRATEGY LINE NOT CATEGORIZED TO A PRODUCT"
=================================================
A false fail, and the name in the finding is the evidence:

  Lemmata Chiropractic - Chiropractic - 35-64/Back

That is the first line of a two-line name. The second line is

  Pain/Neck Pain Behavioral Social Mirror     reachv2

and that gap makes it TWO cells, not one. Only single-cell lines counted as
the tail of a wrapped name, so it was thrown away, the name lost the half
carrying "Social Mirror", and the report was failed for a line item that names
its product perfectly well.

Any line with fewer cells than a data row and no NUMBER in them is name text
now. A two-column total line still gets dropped, because a number in the extra
cells is what makes something a data row rather than a name.


"READS AS CTV, VIDEO TODAY"
=============================
That badge means "the stored product disagrees with what today's code reads
the raw name as", which is worth knowing - it is the whole reason a product
finding can be wrong. It was firing on rows nothing was wrong with, for two
reasons:

  * One line item can sell two products. "CTV + Video Ads" is one buy and two
    rows, so the raw name mapping to CTV AND Video is normal. It was compared
    against the whole list, so a row correctly stored as CTV read as
    disagreeing with itself. The test is now whether the stored product is
    still ONE OF today's answers.

  * It was computed from the MERGED row's raw name, not the line item's own,
    so line 132111 - "Connected TV Ads" - was told it reads as something else
    because a different line item on the same order says "CTV + Video Ads".

When it does fire it now says both halves - "stored as X, reads as Y today" -
with a hover explaining that any product finding on that row is being judged
against a stale answer until the orders are re-read.

TOTALS
=========
A total row under both tables: line items, orders, how many ran in the month,
and the two money columns.

Safe to add up, which is why it can exist at all: a line item selling two
products carries its money against the FIRST of them and nothing against the
second, so a "CTV + Video Ads" buy counts once rather than twice.
