A CAMPAIGN TRAILING OFF IS NOT A MISSING ORDER
=================================================
An order that ran to the last day of a month does not stop dead at midnight. A
few impressions land in the first days of the next one, the serving file counts
them as days served, and the order behind them ended before this month started
- so it is correctly not in the order list, and the client came up as
"delivered with no order behind it" every single month on the way out.

TWO CONDITIONS, AND IT NEEDS BOTH:

  three days or fewer, AND
  the last of them inside the first week of the month

Both, because three days at the END of a month is a campaign that has just
launched - and that one really does need an order behind it, so it stays on the
list. Your Transit of Frederick and NM Chupacabras rows are the first shape;
something starting on the 29th is the second.

The dates come from the serving file itself - first day and last day are kept
per client per month - so this is read off what actually happened rather than
guessed from an order that is not there to look at.

COUNTED, NOT QUIETLY DROPPED. Under the panel:

  4 more served a day or three in the first week and nothing since - a campaign
  that ended delivering the last of itself, so they are not listed above.

And when the tail is all that is left, the panel says so rather than
disappearing, because an empty panel and a panel that has been quietly emptied
look exactly the same.

The rules sheet now carries this, the beta-YYYY rule, and what the panel means,
under "Delivery with no order behind it".
