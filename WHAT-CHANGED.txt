==================================================
Houston Concierge Medicine's Social Mirror is three line items on order 54985
added together: two cancelled at 120,000 each and one live at 100,000. Paced
against all 340,000 the report read 84% short of a goal that two thirds of had
been called off.

53,025 / 100,000 now. Which is 47% short - a real number about a real buy, and
the finding fires on it, because one cancelled line beside a live one is not a
campaign that was called off. Marking the whole product "stopped" had been
silencing the finding on the half still delivering.

This needs the per-line-item detail that arrived in build 88. A row loaded
before that falls back to the merged figures, which is the answer it always

THE ORDER STATUS, BESIDE THE ORDER IT IS ABOUT
=================================================
It sat under the review pill two columns over, where it read as part of the
verdict rather than as the fact behind it - and as words, on a row with none
to spare.

It is a dot beside the order id now. Green live, orange paused, red cancelled,
blue complete, grey anything else, and the words themselves on hover. A row
carrying several statuses gets several dots.


"NO SITE AND APP PERFORMANCE WIDGET" ON A CTV REPORT
=======================================================
KB House of Guns runs BARCK+ on one CTV line item and was FAILED for not
carrying Site and App Performance. CTV does not get one: its inventory is the
publisher list, and Top CTV Publishers was on the report.

The rule already had this exemption for DOOH - a billboard has no site and no
app - and it is the same shape of fact. BARCK+ no longer owes the site-and-app
widget when everything on the report is DOOH, CTV, Social Mirror CTV or Video
AND the report carries the inventory widget those DO owe. That second half is
the point: this is "the breakout it carries is the right one for what ran",
not "CTV reports owe nothing".


A COLUMN OF ZEROES IS NOT A RESULT
=====================================
New check. KB House of Guns' Top CTV Publishers ran 0.00% down every row -
Samsung TV Plus, Philo, Vizio, DIRECTV, Pluto. Nobody watched none of every ad
on all of them in the same month; that is the metric arriving unmapped, and it
goes to the client reading as a campaign that did nothing at all.

Fails when EVERY row of a completion-rate column is zero. One publisher at zero
is ordinary and says nothing.


BROKEN IMAGES
================
The missing-preview check now covers any grid with a picture column, not just
the creative ones - Publisher Image, Station Image, Logo. A publisher logo
coming through as the browser's broken-image glyph is the same failure as a
creative preview that did not render, and the pixel test that finds one finds
the other: a cell holding a real picture has thousands of colors, a broken or
empty one has the table fill and a few marks.

The finding names what it counted - "2 publisher images did not render", "5
creative previews did not render". No new findings on any of the seven fixture
reports.
