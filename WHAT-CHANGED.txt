HTML5 CREATIVES ARE NOT MISSING THEIR PREVIEWS
=================================================
A creative whose name ends in .zip is an HTML5 ad - a bundle of markup and
assets. There is no still frame, so there is nothing to render into the preview
cell and nothing to link to. Both preview checks skipped it before it could be
called a fault:

  Every creative preview rendered        the empty cell on a .zip row is the
                                         format, not a preview that failed

  Every creative variant carries a link  a .zip row is out of BOTH counts, not
                                         just the failing one - "2 of 9 with no
                                         link" has to be 2 of the 9 that could
                                         have had one

Only a trailing .zip, so "Zipline Tours" is a client and stays a client.

Both lines on the Reporting flags tab say so now.


A TICKED BOX SURVIVES A NEW FILE
===================================
You would tick six known false alarms, the corrected pull would come in, and
all six came back. Every month. That is how somebody learns to stop reading
them.

A sign-off is a statement about a particular copy, so a new file still needs a
new one. An acceptance is a statement about a FINDING - "that CTR flag is a
known quirk of this client's GA page" - and it is just as true of the new file
as it was of the old one.

The ticks are carried by what the finding SAYS, not by its position in the
list. So:

  a finding that is still there, word for word    keeps its tick, wherever it
                                                  has moved to in the list
  a finding the new file actually fixed           drops off, and its tick does
                                                  not land on something else
  a finding that is new                           arrives unticked, which is
                                                  the point

Three paths take a new PDF for a report that already exists, and all three had
the same bug: the feed superseding a copy nobody had signed off, "Use the new
file" on a parked arrival, and "Replace the file" by hand. All three carry the
ticks now.

The re-check button already worked this way - this is the same rule applied to
the other three.

One old test asserted the opposite behaviour and has been rewritten to say why
it changed.
