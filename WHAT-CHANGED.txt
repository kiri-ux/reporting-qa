THE ORDER IS THERE. THE IMPORT THREW IT AWAY.
================================================
Order 55476 is IO Live, 1 August to 31 December, client "River Valley Builders
Facebook", partner 7 Mountains PA Selinsgrove. Both tools spell it the same, so
this is not a name problem.

The import keeps line items that touch "the period", and the period defaulted
to THE CALENDAR MONTH BEFORE TODAY. Today is 31 August, so the window it was
loading against was July - and every line item starting on 1 August was dropped
on the way in as "starts after the period".

That is your 118. It is not 118 unrelated problems, it is one: a whole month of
new orders, filtered out by a window a month behind the board. Every one of
them serving 31 days in August is the tell.

The window now comes from the cycle being worked - the same period the board is
showing - rather than from the calendar.


AND IT WOULD HAVE HAPPENED AGAIN ON 1 SEPTEMBER
==================================================
Two reasons, both fixed, because fixing only the first would have left this
waiting to repeat.

A MONTH OF HEADROOM. The import now also keeps orders starting in the month
AFTER the cycle. When the board rolls to September the orders are already here
instead of the same hole opening on the 1st. They are not owed a report until
their month comes round - the board decides that, and it always did.

AND THE CYCLE IS PART OF WHAT "UNCHANGED" MEANS. Nothing re-read the export
when the cycle moved, because the file had not changed and the ETag said there
was nothing to do. So an import that ran under July stayed under July. The
cycle is now in the sync's fingerprint alongside the product mapping, so
changing it forces a re-read - and the sync message says which of the two it
was.

This is the same shape as the TikTok bug in build 82: derived data that keeps
answering from an input nobody noticed had changed.

NOTHING TO PRESS. The fingerprint changed, so the next sync re-reads the export
on its own and the 118 should go with it. If you want it now, "Sync from S3 now"
on the Order list page.
