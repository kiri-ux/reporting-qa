1. Window World - Bowling Green: three findings, one bug
---------------------------------------------------------
All three came from the same parsing fault, and it is worse than the three
findings suggested - it has been quietly corrupting line item names on every
report where a name is long enough to wrap.

TapClicks centres a table row vertically, so a name spread over three lines
prints its NUMBERS on the middle one - the name wraps above its own figures as
well as below. The parser assumed every text line was the tail of the row
above it, which did two things at once:

  - it threw away the first line of every wrapped name, the half carrying the
    client
  - it glued the next row's opening line onto the previous row's name

On this report that put "Amazon CTV" into BOTH line items. Everything else
followed:

  "Line item clicks do not sum to the top line" - both lines read as CTV, so
  both were taken out of the clicks total, leaving 84 clicks "unaccounted for"
  on a report where 84 is the correct answer. Only the Amazon CTV line's 7
  clicks come out; 91 - 7 = 84.

  "Check check_headline_ctr could not run - division by zero" - with every line
  excluded there was nothing left to divide by. A crash is not a verdict; the
  division is guarded now and says "nothing left to divide" instead.

Rows of one grid all open the same way ("<Client> - ..."), so the opening is
learned from the first row and used to cut the block of text between two rows
into the tail of the one above and the head of the one below.

One thing that fell out of fixing it: TapClicks prints a small icon before the
first row of a grid, which comes out of the PDF as a private-use character. It
was harmless while the first line of each name was being discarded. It is
stripped now.


2. Top CTV Publishers on an Amazon-only report
------------------------------------------------
Amazon Premium is bought as "Amazon Premium CTV + Video Ads" and maps to CTV,
so the CTV rule asked for a Top CTV Publishers widget. Amazon does not print
one - its publishers arrive as "Amazon Premium Site and App Performance",
which was on page 8 all along.

The widget is no longer owed when Amazon is the only CTV on the report. A
mixed buy still owes it for the half that is not Amazon.


3. A name, remembered
-----------------------
Type your name once in the top right and it stays on that browser. The name
box disappears from every sign-off row and from the report page; the topbar
shows who you are with a "not you?" to clear it.

It is a cookie, not a login. Nothing is authorised by it and it is not treated
as a security boundary - the board is behind the office network and everyone
on it can already do everything. What sign-off needs is attribution, and
typing it into every row is what made people stop.

Signing off for the first time remembers you, so there is no separate step to
find before the thing you came to do. A typed name still wins over the
remembered one, for signing on behalf of whoever is next to you.


4. Ticking off the last finding signs the report off
------------------------------------------------------
Going through every finding and accepting it IS reading the report. Asking for
a signature afterwards asks the same question twice, and the answer was a
screen of reports at "in, unreviewed" that somebody had in fact been through.

Only on the way in, and only from "new". Un-ticking something does not tear up
a sign-off, and a report already at needs fix or waived has a decision on it
that this will not overwrite. It needs to know who you are, so it does nothing
until a name is saved.


5. The tiles add up what is on screen
---------------------------------------
Filter to one buyer and the counts at the top now follow - expected, not
received, in review, errors, good to go, partners delivered. They were the
whole board's totals regardless, which is the summary answering a question
nobody is asking any more.

The "hours to go" projection steps aside while a filter is on and says "of 710
on the whole board" instead. That rate is measured across the whole cycle;
applied to a subset it would be a number with nothing behind it.


6. The filters live in the URL
--------------------------------
Search text, every dropdown, and the "Only started" toggle now write
themselves into the address bar, so a view can be bookmarked or sent to
somebody. Opening that URL puts the filters back.

replaceState, not pushState - filtering is not navigation, and forty Back
presses to undo one search is not a history. A saved view naming a partner
that is not on this board drops that one rather than showing nothing.


7. No more 1s in the filter menus
-----------------------------------
A count of 1 is never worth printing. It was tested per menu before ("show
them only if any of them varies") and one partner with two cards was enough to
bring the whole column of 1s back. Tested per row, it cannot.
