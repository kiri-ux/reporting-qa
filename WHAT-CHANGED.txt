PACING: WEBSITE VISITOR ID, ADDITIONAL BILLING, SEO AND LIVE CHAT ARE OUT
=========================================================================

entry read "Visitor ID" - which is not what the product is called. The mapping
calls it "Website Visitor ID", so the line meant to keep it out never matched
it, and every order carrying it got a row reading "-/- no comparison".
Additional Billing was not in the list at all. Matching is on the flattened
name now, so a near-miss like that cannot come back quietly.

The total was the worse half. It summed every product in the order, so a goal
with no row above it was still in the denominator - and the served side
counted a flat product's impressions with nothing under them to compare to.
Your 5,414,666/180,000 had a million impressions in it that no row on the
panel was about.

Both sides now count the same products. Where a flat product did print
impressions, the panel says so underneath rather than dropping them silently:

  A further 1,048,919 impressions are on products sold by the month - SEO,

  with nothing to deliver against, so they are not paced and are not in this
  total.

The lifetime "finished under its goal" warning uses the same rule, so it stops
counting flat products on both sides too.


GRAV 51430 - AN ORDER CLOSING OUT BRINGS ITS FINISHED LINES WITH IT
===================================================================
Order 51430 ends 15 August. Its SEO line runs to that date; its Social Mirror
Ads and Website Visitor ID lines stopped on 15 June.

The import drops a line item that ended before the month - which is right for
a monthly and exactly wrong for a lifetime, because a lifetime is a report on
a whole campaign. So the only line of 51430 that survived was SEO, and SEO is
the one product never owed a lifetime. That is where "SEO is not owed a
lifetime" came from: a true sentence about the only line left standing.

A line item that ended before the month is kept now when the ORDER it belongs
to ends inside this cycle's lifetime window. It still does not earn a monthly
- the board decides that off the line's own dates and these did not run in
August. It is there to be reported on.

Grav now reads:

  LIFETIME   Social Mirror   2026-02-15 to 2026-08-15
  SEO        SEO             August, its own file

Which is the two reports you said were owed.

Scope, measured on your export: 167 line items across 64 orders come back.
Not every old line - only the ones on an order that finishes this cycle. A
line that stopped in March under an order running to December is still
dropped, and there is a test holding that.

The orders will re-read themselves on the next sync (the mapping fingerprint
moved), and every report will re-check itself in the background (the rules
fingerprint moved). Nothing to press.
