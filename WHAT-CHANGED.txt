127 - THE PASTED LIST IS KEPT, AND ITS ROWS ARE DECISIONS
============================================================
"Check a list" was a form post and nothing else: paste four hundred rows, read
the comparison, refresh, and it is gone.

The list is kept per cycle now. Come back and it is there, re-run against the
board as it is THEN, which is how this gets used - read it, go and fix three
rows, look again. The page says so under the button, with who pasted it and
when, and there is a "Clear the list" beside it. One list per cycle,
overwritten: it is a copy of somebody else's spreadsheet, not a record.

And every row on "On the list, not on the board" is now a decision with a note
box and two buttons:

  APPROVE  puts the row on this cycle's board with your note as the reason it
           is there - the same override as "Needs a report" on the not-owed
           panel, so it survives every rule underneath it.

  REJECT   settles it. The row stays on the table with the note on it rather
           than reading as an open question next time.

Both record who and when, and both can be undone.

ONE HONEST LIMIT: Approve can only put back a client the ORDER EXPORT knows
about. The board is built from that export, so a client nothing in it carries
cannot have a row created - those are the rows whose reason reads "no order
line carries 51981". The decision is still recorded, and the page says that
under the table rather than letting Approve look like it worked.

The partner comes from the order line, not from your list: the list carries a
partner CODE ("7MOU SG") and the board is keyed on the real name. Matched on
the order id, which is the one thing both sides agree on.


128 - A CLOCK
================
Your question, answered honestly about the build you were on: the breakout
sheet was not synced on a schedule at all. It is read on every order sync, and
the order sync only ever ran when a batch of reports arrived or somebody
pressed the button. Constant during the pull; never on the 12th. The daily
serve file was worse - it lands every morning and nothing was going to look at
it until the next batch.

Every thirty minutes now, all three on one heartbeat, each checked by
fingerprint before anything is read:

  the order export        by ETag - a HEAD request, no download
  the daily serve file    by its own ETag, separately
  the breakout sheet      by a checksum of its own contents

The usual answer is "nothing has changed": three small requests, no writes.
One worker does it, because the sync claims itself and the second worker's
heartbeat finds it taken. A failed check is logged and the next one runs half
an hour later - a heartbeat that stops on one bad answer is worse than none.

SYNC_EVERY_MINUTES changes it, 0 turns it off. The Partners page states the
interval, and the sync log now has words for this trigger so a sync nobody
started is not a mystery.
