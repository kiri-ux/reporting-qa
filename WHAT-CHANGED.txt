CHECK A LIST NOW FILTERS, AND STARTS WITH THE REJECTIONS PUT AWAY
=================================================================
Rejecting a row is supposed to settle it. A hundred and twenty-five settled
rows were still sitting above the ones nobody has answered yet.

  Show  [Undecided 8]  [Approved 3]  [Rejected 114]  |  [Worth a look 6]

Rejected starts off. The chip says how many and puts them back. "Worth a look"
is a second, independent narrowing - the rows the board has no good answer for
- so you can point it at those regardless of what has been decided.

It filters in the browser, not on the server. Every decision on that page is
already saved without redrawing it, because redrawing means rebuilding the
board off every order line and re-parsing your pasted list - the best part of
a minute. A filter that reloaded would give that back.

One deliberate exception: a row you have just decided stays on screen. Rejected
is hidden, so rejecting one would make it vanish under the cursor, which reads
as the click having gone somewhere else. It stays until you touch the filter
or reload.


VICTORINO'S PIZZERIA 52819 - THE NOTE WAS MAKING A CLAIM IT COULD NOT MAKE
==========================================================================
It said:

  the export has this order and every line on it ended 2026-07-15, before
  August 2026

There are two lines on that order. One ended 15 July. The other is the Social
Mirror pending launch dated 1 September, sitting right there in the IO tool.

The import recorded ONE reason per order and then, at the end of the file,
rewrote it with the latest end date - so the second line's reason was
overwritten by the first line's date, and a sentence saying "every line on it"
was built out of one of them.

Every distinct reason is kept now, the date rewrite happens in place instead of
replacing the entry, and when there is more than one the sentence says the
thing that is true of the whole order:

  no line on this order was running in August 2026 - one ended 2026-07-15,
  another is IO Pending Launch: Element(s) Missing, starting 2026-09-01

A pending line also says WHEN it starts now. "IO Paused, IO Pending Launch"
leaves you wondering whether it might have run anyway; "starting 2026-09-01"
does not.
