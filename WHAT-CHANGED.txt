--------------------------------------------------------
You have, and the reason is worth more than another point fix.

Your screenshot shows Investigate saying:

    Social Mirror · order 31449   2025-01-03 to 2026-12-31 · counted

That is ONE window. The fix in build 42 records each order's own flight
separately, so a real re-read of that order would show TWO - one ending 30 June
and one starting 1 August, with July in the gap. One window means this row was

what turned one bug into three rounds of screenshots.
