"ON THE REPORT WITH NO LIVE ORDER: MOBILE CONQUESTING"
========================================================
Big J Mobile Homes has a line item called

  Big J Mobile Homes - Manufactured Homes/Mobile Home Dealer/Retargeting
  Performance Max

which wraps over two lines in the PDF. The second line - "Home Dealer/
Retargeting Performance Max" - carries the word Performance, and the table
parser treated ANY line carrying a widget word as the next widget's title. So
it was thrown away, and what was left of the name ended in "Mobile".

Which is the Mobile Conquesting tail. The report was failed for carrying a
product with no live order, on the strength of half a name.

The widget word now has to END the line, which is what makes a title a title.
"Mobile Conquesting Creative Performance" still ends a table; "... Retargeting
Performance Max" is a name, and it is now kept.


"2 CREATIVE PREVIEWS DID NOT RENDER" WHEN ONE HAD
====================================================
Two things, both in the pixel test that shipped in build 93.

A FLAT PICTURE IS STILL A PICTURE. Northeast Texas Community College's
creatives come through as a solid navy band clipped to a few points tall - two
or three colours in the whole cell - and "an empty cell has almost no colours"
called them empty. A cell with few colours is now asked the other question:
how much of it is NOT the background. A few per cent is a table border; a
quarter of it in navy is something printed there.

THE WHOLE CELL, NOT A BAND AROUND THE NUMBER. The row's figures are centred
vertically and its picture is not - a short logo sits at the TOP of a tall row
- so a symmetric slice around the number could miss it. Halfway to the row
above, halfway to the row below: that is the cell, whatever shape it is.


"1 PRODUCT WITH NO COMPLETION RATE", ON PAGE 2
================================================
Two things wrong and the page number was the tell: a completion rate is never
printed on Line Item Performance, so nobody would look for one there.

Trinity Valley Community College prints "Social Mirror CTV Creative
Performance" as a widget title and "Video Completion Rate" as a column header
underneath it. The check wanted the product name and the word "Completion" on
ONE LINE, and they are never on one line - so the report was failed for a rate
printed two lines below the name it was being looked for beside. 98.69% and
98.76%, right there on page 8.

A widget is titled after its product and its figures are inside it, so the
question is now whether the product's OWN WIDGET carries a completion rate.
The tile shape - "CTV Completion Rate" alone on a line - still counts.

And when it really is missing, the finding points at the product's widget,
where it should have been, rather than at the first line mentioning the product
at all.
