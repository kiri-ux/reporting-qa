1. The client link is at the top now
--------------------------------------

the page for - the link you were about to send - was found by scrolling.

partner delivered this cycle with an Open button and a Copy button. It is
built from the deliveries rather than from the partner list, so a partner
whose orders shift after its reports went out does not take its link off the
page with it.

Copy falls back to the old execCommand path when the clipboard API is not
available - the board is opened over plain http on the office network, where
that API is simply absent, and a button that silently does nothing is worse
than one that says "Press Ctrl+C".
