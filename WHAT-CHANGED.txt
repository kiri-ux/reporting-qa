1. A product is only expected if it ran THAT month
----------------------------------------------------
Blair Regional YMCA, order 31449. Social Mirror CTV ran to 30 June 2026 and
starts again on 1 August. Two separate orders.

The order list keeps one row per client and product with the WIDEST span
across every order - first start to last end. That is the right answer for
"when does this campaign end", and completely wrong for "was it running in
July": merged, those two flights are one campaign from 2025 to December 2026,
and July - a month in which no Social Mirror CTV ran at all - sits inside it.

So the July report was failed twice for it. Once for the product, once for the
Top CTV Publishers widget the product would have owed.

Each order's own window is now kept alongside the merged span, and "did it run
in July" is asked of the individual windows. The merged span is still there
and still answers the end-date question.

Order rows loaded before this fall back to the old behaviour rather than
guessing, and are replaced on the next order sync.


2. "CTV + Video Ads" is two products
--------------------------------------
Bloomsburg Chevrolet, order 43852. One line item, product "CTV + Video Ads",
and a report carrying a CTV section and a Video section - which is exactly
what was bought. Read as CTV alone, the Video was a product with no live
order.

" + " in a product name is the IO tool's word for "and", and both sides now
map. Two things it deliberately does not split:

  - "YouTube+ Video Ads" - the plus belongs to the name. Splitting that puts a
    live YouTube order back under Video, which is the bug this file exists for.
    Only " + " with spaces around it counts.
  - "Display & Video Ads" - an ampersand is the FORMAT a product is delivered
    in, not a second product. "Meta Display & Video Ads" is Meta.

There is a test that walks a product name through six format tails and expects
the same answer each time.


3. Saved filter views
-----------------------
Name the filters you are looking at and they become a chip under the filter
row - click to load, x to forget. Saving the same name again replaces it.

The period is deliberately NOT saved. A view built while looking at July should
open on whatever cycle you are on, or it is wrong the moment the month turns.
Only known filter keys are stored, since the query comes from the browser.

The chip for the view you are currently on is highlighted.
