FIRST NAMES
==============
Everywhere a person is named: the B / R / T / S tags on a partner card, the
Reporter column, the sign-off beside it, the filter dropdowns, and the workload
page. "Lauren Hunter" reads as "Lauren", because that is what anybody here would
say out loud and the card is already carrying a partner name, a percentage, a
progress bar and six state pills.

THE ROLES KEEP THE TWO KATIES APART. A name is only ever read inside its role -
the buyer tag, the trainer tag, one tab each on the workload page - so the
trainer Katie and the buyer Katie are never in the same list to be confused for
each other. Nothing merges them.

Where it would be ambiguous, the surname stays. Two people in the SAME role
sharing a first name - a buyer Katie Oxman and a buyer Katie Reed - keep their
surnames, both of them, because a label that cannot tell two people apart is
worse than a long one.

AND THE OTHER WAY ROUND, WHICH IS THE ONE THAT WAS COSTING YOU SOMETHING. The
sheet spells the same person two ways: "Lauren" on one partner, "Lauren Hunter"
on another. The workload page counted those as two people with half a workload
each. A clash is now two different SURNAMES on one first name, not two
spellings - so Lauren is one person again, and Katie Oxman and Katie Reed would
still both keep theirs.

"Todd, Megan" in one field stays two names, shortened individually - "Todd
Sanders, Megan Hill" reads as "Todd, Megan".

WHAT IS STORED DOES NOT CHANGE. A sign-off keeps whatever the person typed,
because that is the record of who said so; the first name is only how it reads
on the row. Same for the roster: the sheet is untouched.
