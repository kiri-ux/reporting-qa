1. A paused line item makes no claim either way
-------------------------------------------------
W&L Subaru's only Meta line is IO Paused and ended 30 June, and the July report
was failed for a missing Meta section.

A paused buy is not delivering, so it is not owed on the report. And if its
product does turn up anyway, that is not a surprise either - the buy exists, it
is just not running. Both directions, as you asked.

An order line counts as live if ANY line item behind it is, so a client running
one product across a live order and a paused one still owes it.

Investigate says which it was:

    Meta · order 14885    2020-08-10 to 2026-06-30 · paused, so not owed either way


2. Amazon Premium: CTV OR Video, not both
-------------------------------------------
"Amazon Premium CTV + Video Ads" is one line item that can put all of its
impressions through one half, so Window World's July - CTV and no Video - is a
normal Amazon month rather than a missing product.

Both halves are now ONE expectation with two acceptable answers. Either
satisfies it, both are allowed on the report, and if NEITHER turns up that is
still a finding.

"CTV + Video Ads" without Amazon is unchanged: that buy runs both, and
Bloomsburg Chevrolet's report carries both.
