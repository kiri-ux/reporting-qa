which needed both budgets present and their ratio to come out whole. That is

AND THE MONEY IS THE VALIDATOR NOW, WHICH IS WHERE YOU PUT IT. total budget

208,140 rows that carry both. The 58 that disagree mean one of the two fields
is wrong on that line, and the sync record now says how many:

  ... 3 line item(s) where the months on the order and the budgets disagree
  about how long the campaign runs

The campaign total is built on months_running - the order's own field - and the
budgets only ever get to raise a hand.
