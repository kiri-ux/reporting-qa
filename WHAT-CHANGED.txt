1. "6 unrecognised devices" that were not devices
---------------------------------------------------
R&R Heating's device table has two rows, Connected TV and Streaming Device,
both real. The six it complained about - Publisher Image, Plex, TCL Channel,
Sling TV, Tubi - are the TOP CTV PUBLISHERS widget underneath it, plus one of
its column headers.

The boundary that is supposed to stop a widget at the next widget's title
anchored on a title starting in COLUMN ONE. pdftotext -layout indents the whole
page, so it never matched anything - and every widget block ran on until it hit
the page footer or a fixed character limit. On a report where the device table
is short and the publishers widget starts right after it, that meant reading
one widget's rows as the other's.

This is not only the device check. Every rule that reads a widget block - the
completion rates, among others - was working with the same runaway boundary and
is now bounded correctly.


2. A DOOH-only report does not owe a site and app breakout
------------------------------------------------------------
A billboard has no site and no app. There is nothing for that widget to list
and TapClicks does not print one, so it is no longer owed when DOOH is the only
product on the report. A report running DOOH alongside anything else still owes
it for the rest.
