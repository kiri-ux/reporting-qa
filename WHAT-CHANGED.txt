TOTAL IMPRESSIONS PER LINE ITEM - THE EXPORT DOES NOT HAVE IT
=============================================================
Short answer: the number is not in the file, and I checked two ways before
saying so.

The export HAS a total_campaign_impressions column. Four of them, in fact - the
header repeats - and the one that is populated holds "1". It is a share of
goal, not a count. There is already a guard throwing that away, which is why
the Campaign total column has only ever shown money: the money column is real,
the impressions one is not.

So the only other route is adding up the months, and I built that: the monthly
impressions figure IS real, and summing it across a line item's months gives a
campaign total. It is careful about the two traps - it counts before the
duplicate test, because that test discards the rows carrying the other months,
and it keys on the month, because 95% of your file is rows repeated verbatim.

THEN I RAN IT OVER YOUR EXPORT AND IT PRODUCED NOTHING, for a reason worth
knowing: of the 10,597 line items in that file, NOT ONE has more than a single
dated row. Every line item's date is pinned to the month it started, which is
the same thing that makes narrowing the date range so dangerous. There are no
months to add up.

The code is in and it is honest about this: a campaign total is only claimed
where more than one month actually contributed. One month gets a dash rather
than a number that would read as the whole flight and be a twelfth of it. If a
wider export ever carries per-month rows, the column fills in on its own.

If that figure exists somewhere else - TapClicks itself, or a different export
- point me at it and I will read it from there.
