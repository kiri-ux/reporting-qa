CLINTON PARKWAY NURSERY - THE DATE WAS OFF SOMEBODY ELSE'S ORDER
================================================================
Order 55048 is one Meta line, IO Live, 25 August to 31 October. The page said

  nothing starts until 2026-09-12, after this cycle

with the IO tool open on screen saying 25 August. That date was never 55048's.

Two things put it there, and both are the same mistake in different places.

An order line row is one client and one product rolled up across every order
that sells it, and its dates are the WIDEST of them - so quoting them at
somebody asking about ONE order gives a date that may belong to a different
one. The lifetime reasons were fixed for this a few builds back; the two date
reasons had not been. They read each order's own line items now.

And when the order id matches nothing loaded at all, the lookup falls back to
the client's other orders. That is the right thing to do - it is how a client
whose orders spell their name differently still gets an answer - but every
sentence after it still said "this order" about somebody else's. It says which
now:

  no order line carries 55048 - the client's other orders do not start until
  2026-09-12, after this cycle

Which is a different thing to go and check, and true.
