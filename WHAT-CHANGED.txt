1. Sign off several reports at once
-------------------------------------
Every row in the still-open table now has a checkbox, and a bar above the table
does the rest:

  Select the ones that passed   ticks every visible row with nothing open
  Clear selection
  3 selected                    Mark reviewed   Mark needs fix

Selected rows highlight, and the header checkbox takes everything on screen.

Three things it is careful about:

  - ONLY WHAT IS ON SCREEN. Select-all and "the ones that passed" both skip
    rows a filter is hiding. Signing off something nobody was looking at is
    exactly what a bulk action should never do.
  - The ids are collected at submit, not rendered per row, so a checkbox in a
    filtered-out row cannot be posted with the rest.
  - "The ones that passed" reads whether the report has anything OPEN, not the
    status pill. A report can sit amber on a warning somebody has already
    ticked off, and that one has passed.

It needs to know who you are, same as any sign-off. With a name saved there is
no name box; without one it asks, and remembers.


2. The device warning starts at 20%
-------------------------------------
"Device totals 15,066 against 17,535 eligible (-14.1%)" was a warning at the
old 10% line. A device breakout runs a little under the eligible total on most
reports - unknown-device filtering alone accounts for ten or twelve points - so
that threshold was firing on the ordinary case, which is how a warning stops
being read at all.
