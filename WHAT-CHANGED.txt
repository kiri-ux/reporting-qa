THE GA PAGES WERE BEING READ AS A CTR TABLE
==============================================
Both of those are one bug.

A table ran until it hit the next line carrying three metric labels. A grid
ran until it hit a section banner or something that looked like the next
widget's title. Neither of those is on a Google Analytics page: the GA4 titles
are centered, and an indented line reads as the tail of a wrapped row name
rather than a title, and not one report in the fixtures prints a section
banner at all.

So the GA pages were not skipped. They were read as MORE ROWS of a table
several pages earlier, with their numbers matched to that table's columns by
where they sit on the line:

  Eastern p19, GA4 by Geo    Sessions -> impressions, Views -> clicks,
                             Engagement rate -> CTR. Five "Row CTR does not
                             match its own numbers", every one of them
                             "204/204 = 100.000%" against a printed 1.47%.

  Dominion p13, GA4 summary  733 read as clicks, 391 as impressions. FAILED
                             for "1 site clicking above 5%" - 187.47% - on a
                             page with no CTR column anywhere on it.

A widget lives on a page. The report header block prints at the top of every
page and nowhere else, so that is the boundary now, in both the table parser
and the grid reader. A widget that carries on over the page repeats its own
title and starts again there, which is what it already did.

This was quietly costing accuracy elsewhere too. "minefun.io" was stored as
"minefun.io Chats: Number of people that started", "6471 County Hwy-625" as
"6471 County Hwy-625 Device", and four fixtures had a site named
"radio-en-vivo.mx Powered by TCPDF (www.tcpdf.org)". All gone.


3 PREVIEWS THAT DID NOT RENDER, ON A TABLE WITH 2
====================================================
It counted every "Thumbnail not available" in the whole document and then
pinned the total to wherever the first one happened to be. Eastern's Social
Mirror table has two; the third was in a different widget on a different page.

Counted against the table it names, it read as plainly wrong - and a number
you cannot check against the page in front of you is worse than no number.

One finding per widget now, each counting only its own. Accepting one no
longer hides another.


WHERE PACING WENT
====================
It has been dead since build 82, on every report, not just these.

  from .roster import client_flight, ordered_for

client_flight is in ingest.py. roster has never had it. So every report raised
ImportError on that line, and the panel is wrapped in a bare "except Exception"
- a pacing panel is never worth a 500 - which turned that into an empty panel.
Nothing said so, because a panel that fails looks exactly like a panel with
nothing to say.

Fixed, and the swallow is no longer silent: the exception is logged, and the
page now says why the panel is empty instead of vanishing. Four things stop it
and each needs a different thing done about it:

  no order line matches this client
  none of this client's N order lines ran in 2026-07
  every line item on this client's orders is paused or cancelled
  the orders carry no monthly budget and no impression goal

and, if it ever breaks again, "the pacing panel could not be built
(ImportError)" rather than silence.
