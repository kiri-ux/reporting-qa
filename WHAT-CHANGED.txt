PARAGON CASINO RESORT - A GOAL MORE THAN HALF OF WHICH WAS CALLED OFF
=====================================================================
Order 55583 is two Social Mirror line items: 134958 cancelled at 97,500 and
134957 live at 85,000. The monthly panel has taken cancelled lines out for a
while. The lifetime panel had not - it was still reading the rolled-up row,
which is every line item added together - so it paced 86,573 against 182,500
and called it 53% short.

Against the 85,000 still being asked for, that report is slightly over.

Cancelled lines are out of the goal on lifetimes now, the same as on monthlies,
and the "N months at the monthly figure" line names the months actually
multiplied rather than the merged span across a cancelled July line and a live
August one.


AND THE SAME SENTENCE WAS BEING SAID TWICE
==========================================
  Social Mirror is 53% short - 86,573 served against 182,500 ordered
  Campaign finished 53% under its goal - 86,573 against 182,500 sold

Two warnings, one number. On a lifetime the campaign check reports the whole
campaign and the product check reports a product, and when the campaign IS one
product those are the same fact. The campaign line keeps it - it is the point
of a lifetime, it carries the goal's basis and it says which page. It only ever
reports UNDER, so a product that over-delivers still gets its own line.


CANCELLED LINES FOLD AWAY ON THE ORDER PANEL
============================================
A cancelled line is out of every goal on the report and is not owed on it, so a
red pill in the middle of the list reads as the finding when it is the one row
nothing is being decided from. It is behind "N cancelled line items" now, with
its money still on it - which half was called off is exactly what somebody
opens that panel to find out.


ADDED BY HAND, ON THE ROW ITSELF
================================
The panel listing every hand-added row is gone. You were right that it is a
second board to read: those rows are already on the board below it.

Instead:

  * every row somebody put on the cycle carries a BY HAND tag beside the
    client, with who and why on hover - on every row, not only the ones still
    waiting for a report
  * a filter beside the report search, "Added by hand N", narrows to just them
    and works alongside the search and the column dropdowns rather than
    fighting them
  * "Add a row to this cycle" is a fold-down above the reports:

      Partner (dropdown) | Client | Order | Report (monthly/lifetime/SEO)
      Products (multi-select) | Why | Save

The partner is picked, not typed - the board is keyed on the partner's real
name and one spelled a hair differently groups with nothing. The products go
onto the row, so it names its buy like every other row and the product checks
have something to judge the PDF against. The order number too, so searching it
finds the row.

Approving from the list check writes the same thing, so your approves carry the
same tag and appear in the same filter.
