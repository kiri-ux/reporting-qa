Read every {{ sync_every }} minutes, and on every order sync - whichever comes first - and only re-loaded when the sheet has actually changed, so an untouched sheet costs one request and no writes. It is read-only - nothing here is ever written back, so the sheet stays the source of truth for who owns what.
Two guards. Where a partner takes delivery is set here, not always in the sheet, so a target the sheet does not name is kept rather than blanked - a blank means Drive, and that hands a Dropbox partner's client the wrong link. And a read that comes back much shorter than the roster already loaded is refused: a sign-in page and a renamed tab both parse perfectly well as a roster with nobody in it.

Replace the roster by hand

Export the reporting roster tab as CSV and upload it here. The headers it expects are the sheet's own: Partner, Buyer, Email, SEO, Email, Manager, Reporting Team, To:, Trainer, Reporting Notes, Buyer Notes - the two Email columns are read in order, the first as the buyer's and the second as the SEO person's.
A copy ships with the app, so a fresh deploy always has a roster. Uploading here replaces it and survives future deploys.
