Budgets

This one merges, it does not replace. A sheet covering a single product only fills in the money columns on the lines it names - it never deletes an order and never adds one. That is what makes it safe to drop in a Performance Max export without losing everything else.
It reads Monthly Campaign Budget, and the per-product spend columns for Meta, PPC and LinkedIn. Rows are matched on line item id, falling back to order id. Anything in the sheet that is not on the board yet is counted and reported rather than quietly dropped.
