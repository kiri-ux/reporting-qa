What this tool asks for, what it does not, and what happens after. Every rule here is one the board actually applies - if the two ever disagree, the board is wrong.

A monthly is owed when

  • a client had at least one product delivering during the reporting month,
  • and that product ran {{ min_days }} days or more of it. A campaign that started on the 30th waits for next month - two days of delivery is a page of near-zero numbers that reads as a broken report.
  • One product running a full week is enough. A second product starting on the 30th does not excuse the rest of the campaign.

A lifetime is owed when

  • an order finishes inside the cycle - month end, plus a few days to the 3rd business day, so a campaign that ended on the 1st ships with the monthlies rather than waiting a month.
  • It is never held back for a short run. A campaign that ended on the 2nd ran its whole flight; that only two of those days fall in this cycle says nothing about the report it owes.
  • Unless the order that ended overlaps another order still running. The client is not dark - the buy changed shape - and a campaign-to-date report in the middle of a live campaign is not what a lifetime is for. An order that starts after the ended one finished does not count as an overlap, so that client does get their lifetime.
  • Unless a lifetime has already gone out for that campaign. If the end date moves out after one was sent, the client goes back on the normal schedule and owes a new lifetime when it finishes for real.

A campaign shorter than {{ short_days }} days

  • gets its lifetime only, when it ends in the same month it started. The first monthly and the lifetime would cover the same days and print the same numbers.
  • A client who has had monthlies before is mid-relationship, so this only applies to a campaign's first month.

What a report is checked against

  • A monthly is judged against the month: the products live during it, the budget for it, and a date range that reads exactly month start to month end.
  • A lifetime is judged against the campaign: every product that ran during its flight, the whole campaign's goal, and a date range that matches the order's own start and end.
  • A finished campaign that delivered more than {{ goal_band }}% under its goal is flagged - not as a failure, because the goal is often the monthly figure across the flight rather than a total the order states.

Names and delivery

  • Every report is filed as July 2026_Client 53908.pdf, or Lifetime_Client 53908.pdf - built from the cycle, the client the report itself prints, and the order ids. A file that arrived without its order id is renamed and says so on the report.
  • A partner is packaged when every one of its reports is signed off. Where it goes - Drive or Dropbox - comes from the reporting roster, and the links page says if a delivery went somewhere else.
  • Signing off is a person saying they read this answer. It survives a re-check that changes nothing they would have cared about, and is pulled when a failure appears that was not there when they signed.
