{# The rules the board applies. Stated, not explained - the reasoning lives in the code, and a rule somebody is checking against does not need it. #}

A monthly is owed

  • when a product delivered for {{ min_days }} days or more of the reporting month.
  • One product meeting that is enough for the client's report.
  • Under {{ min_days }} days: not owed. It waits for next month.

A lifetime is owed

  • when an order ends inside the cycle - the reporting month through the 3rd business day of the next one.
  • Short runs are not exempt. An order that ended on the 2nd owes one.
  • Not owed if the ended order overlaps an order still running.
  • Not owed if that campaign's lifetime has already gone out. If its end date moves out afterwards, it is owed again when the campaign ends.

A campaign of {{ short_days }} days or less

  • owes its lifetime only, when it ends in the month it started and has had no monthly before.

What each report is checked against

  • Monthly: the products live that month, that month's budget, and a date range of month start to month end.
  • Lifetime: the products that ran during the campaign, the whole campaign's goal, and a date range matching the order's start and end.
  • A finished campaign more than {{ goal_band }}% under its goal is flagged.

Names

  • Monthly: July 2026_Client 53908.pdf
  • Lifetime: Lifetime_Client 53908.pdf
  • A file that arrives without its order id is renamed and flagged.

Delivery

  • A partner is packaged once every one of its reports is signed off.
  • Drive or Dropbox is set per partner on the Partners page.
  • A sign-off is pulled when a re-check finds a failure that was not there when it was signed.
