A monthly is owed

  • when a product delivered for {{ min_days }} days or more of the reporting month.
  • The report covers the whole client, so if any one of its products hits {{ min_days }} days, the client is owed a report - the others do not have to.
  • Under {{ min_days }} days: not owed. It waits for next month.
  • Not every product earns one. Website Visitor ID and Additional Billing are billed and never appear on a report. Live Chat does appear on one, but is only ever sold alongside another digital product - so a client running nothing but those is not owed a report, and a client running Live Chat with something else has it on that report.

A lifetime is owed

  • when an order ends inside the cycle - the reporting month through the 3rd business day of the next one.
  • How long it ran does not matter. An order that ended on the 2nd still owes a lifetime.
  • Not owed if the ended order overlaps an order still running.
  • Not owed if that campaign's lifetime has already gone out. If its end date moves out later, it is owed again when the campaign ends.

A campaign of {{ short_days }} days or less

  • owes its lifetime only, when it ends in the month it started and has had no monthly before.

Delivery with no order behind it

  • A client that delivered this month with no order line loaded is named on the Order list page - that is what a partner's export failing to land looks like.
  • Not when it served three days or fewer and all of them in the first week. An order that ran to the last day of a month keeps delivering for a day or two into the next one; the order behind that is finished and is correctly not on the list.
  • Not a client whose name carries beta-YYYY. Those are trial records and will never have an order.

What each report is checked against

  • Monthly: the products live that month, that month's budget, and a date range of month start to month end.
  • Lifetime: the products that ran during the campaign, the whole campaign's goal, and a date range matching the order's start and end.
  • A finished campaign more than {{ goal_band }}% under its goal is flagged.

Names

  • Monthly: July 2026_Client 53908.pdf
  • Lifetime: Lifetime_Client 53908.pdf
  • A file that arrives without its order id is renamed and flagged.

Delivery

  • A partner is packaged once every one of its reports is signed off.
  • Drive or Dropbox is set per partner on the Partners page.
  • A sign-off is pulled when a re-check finds a failure that was not there when it was signed.
